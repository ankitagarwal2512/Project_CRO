### 3. Featured: AI Interviewer (new section)

- **Eyebrow:** New · AI Interviewer
- **H2:** Screen 200 candidates in 2 hours.
- **Sub:** Run structured AI interviews with many candidates at once. Get scores, transcripts, and a ranked shortlist on one dashboard.

**How it works (4 steps):**
1. **Create the job.** Add the role, must-have skills, and level. No JD? AI drafts one, or parses the PDF you already have.
2. **Configure the round once.** Set duration, rubric, and question style. AI writes role-specific questions from your skills. About 5 minutes of setup.
3. **Share the link.** Every candidate interviews in parallel, 24×7, at a time they choose.
4. **Review the scored report.** Overall score, a skill-by-skill rubric, and a Hire / No-hire recommendation, with AI-written reasons that quote what the candidate said.

**Nothing is taken on trust:** Full transcript · Audio and video recording, synced to the transcript · Proctoring (periodic screenshots, tab-switch and identity checks)

**Proof stats:** 92% screening time saved per role · 18 → 6 days to hire at Uneecops · 24×7 interviews

**Pricing line:** ₹99 per interview. No subscription. Start with 5 free credits (75 minutes of real interviews). No card required.
**CTAs:** Start Free · Book a Demo

### 9. FAQ (rewrite)

1. **What is One AI?** HROne's AI layer, with three agents: Analytics (MIS answers and charts), Employee (does 20+ HR tasks by text or voice), and Policy (answers from your HR policies). It also powers AI features in every module.
2. **What can the Employee Agent actually do?** Apply leave, mark attendance, regularize attendance, apply On Duty or Short Leave, raise helpdesk tickets, and more. It completes the task instead of explaining the steps.
3. **Does One AI work with voice?** Yes. One AI is India's first voice-powered AI agent for HR.
4. **Does the Policy Agent use our own policies?** Yes. It answers from the policy documents you upload and shows which policy each answer came from.
5. **What is AI Interviewer and how much does it cost?** It runs structured first-round interviews with many candidates at once and returns scored, proctored, transcribed reports. ₹99 per 15-minute interview, with volume discounts. 5 free credits to start.
6. **How is AI Interviewer kept fair and cheat-proof?** Every candidate is scored on the same rubric, and every score cites the transcript. Proctoring logs screenshots, tab switches, and identity checks.
7. **How is One AI priced?** Pay only for what you use.
8. **Is our data secure?** *(Pull from the Infosec slide, slide 11.)*
9. **Where can employees use One AI?** Wherever they already work: the HROne web app, the mobile app, WhatsApp, and Microsoft Teams. The agents work in every one of them.
10. **Do employees need training?** No. If they can type or speak a question, they can use One AI.
11. **Can ChatGPT replace an HR AI agent?** ChatGPT can explain how to apply leave. One AI applies it, inside your HRMS, using your policies and your data.
